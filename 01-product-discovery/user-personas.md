# User Personas

## 1. The Guest (The Disconnected Vacationer)
* **Goal:** Maximize their vacation experience without constant effort or planning.
* **Pain Point:** Hates walking to the reception just to ask "What time is the campfire?" or missing an event because the time changed.
* **Tech Tolerance:** Low friction only. Will scan a QR code, but will *not* download an app or create a complex account.

## 2. The Resort Manager (The Revenue Driver)
* **Goal:** Increase ancillary revenue (spa, F&B, paid tours) and ensure smooth operations.
* **Pain Point:** Has no way to push a "15% off Spa" offer to guests who just finished a grueling 3-hour trek. 
* **Tech Tolerance:** Medium. Needs a simple, fast dashboard to manage schedules. Hates bloated, legacy enterprise software.

## 3. The Front Desk Staff (The Orchestrator)
* **Goal:** Serve guests efficiently without being overwhelmed.
* **Pain Point:** Spends 40% of their shift answering the exact same question: "Is the plantation walk still happening?"
* **Tech Tolerance:** Needs a tool that takes seconds to update a schedule or trigger a notification.
