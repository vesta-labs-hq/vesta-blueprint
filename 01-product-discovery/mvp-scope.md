# MVP Scope & Boundaries

To ensure a rapid time-to-market and maintain our "lightweight" value proposition against bloated enterprise competitors, the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) is strictly scoped.

## ✅ In Scope (The Core Engine)
1. **Frictionless Onboarding:** QR-code access to a React-based Guest PWA.
2. **Dynamic Digital Itinerary:** A live feed of today's activities.
3. **RSVP & Capacity Management:** Guests can tap to join; Admins can cap headcounts.
4. **Real-Time Push Notifications:** One-click broadcasts for delays or venue changes.
5. **Targeted Offers Module:** Admins can push simple digital coupons (e.g., "10% off Cafe") to active guests.

## ❌ Out of Scope (For MVP)
1. **Room Booking Engine:** We do not handle room reservations.
2. **Payment Gateway Integration:** RSVPs are free or billed to the room via the front desk manually. (Payments will be added in v2).
3. **PMS Integration:** No automated sync with legacy Property Management Systems (Oracle, Cloudbeds). Vesta operates as a standalone orchestration layer.
