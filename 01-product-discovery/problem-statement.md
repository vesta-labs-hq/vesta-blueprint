# Problem Statement & Hypothesis

## The Trigger (Yercaud, India)
During a family stay at a mid-tier resort, we discovered a systemic operational flaw: **Activity discovery is broken.** The resort offered excellent activities (trekking, magic shows, coffee walks), but relied on static, printed room flyers.

## The Core Problems
1. **The Information Bottleneck:** Front desk staff cannot instantly broadcast schedule changes (e.g., rain delays) to all guests.
2. **Missed Ancillary Revenue:** Because guests aren't proactively notified about activities or spa availability, they miss out—and the resort loses secondary revenue.
3. **The App Friction:** Guests refuse to download a native iOS/Android app from the App Store for a 2-day vacation.

## The Hypothesis
If we provide resorts with a lightweight **Event Orchestration & Upselling Engine**—delivered directly to guests via a frictionless QR-code Progressive Web App (PWA)—we will increase guest participation, drive secondary revenue, and eliminate front-desk bottlenecks.
